## about

I build backend systems and AI pipelines, and I turn messy operational data into automated reporting that people actually trust.

I started by pulling Excel sheets apart. That grew into automation (VBA workbooks ported to Apps Script, crawlers that enrich thousands of rows) and then full applications: FastAPI services, RAG assistants and desktop tools.

My background spans data analysis in a high-stakes aviation environment and hands-on backend/AI engineering, including shipping a production RAG system for a real client. Outside of work I'm usually deep in AI research, financial markets, music recommendation or football modelling, or looking for new ways to tell stories with data.
